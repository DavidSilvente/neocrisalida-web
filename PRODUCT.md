# NeoCrisálida — Product Context

## Product

NeoCrisálida is a Spanish-language online psychology and emotional-support website led by Carmen.

The website exists to give NeoCrisálida a professional digital presence that preserves the emotional identity already visible in its Instagram communication while significantly improving:

- credibility;
- visual coherence;
- perceived quality;
- clarity;
- differentiation;
- usability.

This is a public-facing website.

It is not an application, dashboard or authenticated product.

# Primary audiences

NeoCrisálida has two first-class audiences.

## Audience 1 — Person going through a difficult emotional period

Current public communication explicitly references:

- duelo y pérdidas;
- crisis vitales;
- crisis existenciales;
- necesidades emocionales;
- estrés;
- preocupación;
- burnout;
- cansancio emocional;
- soledad;
- sensación de vacío;
- bloqueo;
- desmotivación.

The website should make this person feel:

- understood;
- welcomed;
- not judged;
- able to slow down;
- able to recognize themselves in the situations described;
- that there is a real person available to listen.

Do not dramatize suffering for visual impact.

Do not use manipulative fear or sadness.

---

## Audience 2 — Relative or close person supporting someone who is suffering

This is NOT a secondary marketing line.

It is a real differentiating part of NeoCrisálida's existing communication.

Current public messaging includes:

> "Si tienes a alguien cercano que está sufriendo y no sabes cómo ayudarle, también puedo acompañarte."

And:

> "Te ayudo a comprender, sostener y cuidar de ti mientras acompañas."

The website should give this audience a clear place in the experience.

Do not hide this inside a generic services list.

The website should help a visitor understand:

# Pinned brand commitment — Butterfly journey

This is a USER-PINNED creative direction.

It is NOT optional.

The NeoCrisálida website must use the butterfly / chrysalis journey as a recurring visual protagonist of the experience.

The governing visual journey is:

CRISÁLIDA / CONTAINMENT

↓

TRANSFORMATION

↓

OPENING

↓

EMERGENCE

↓

BUTTERFLY / FLIGHT

The website experience itself should express that progression.

The visitor should be able to feel that the visual world is transforming as they move through the page.

The butterfly must participate in that journey.

---

## What this does NOT mean

This does NOT mean:

- putting butterfly icons everywhere;
- repeating decorative butterflies in every section;
- creating a childish butterfly theme;
- creating a mystical/spiritual butterfly website;
- using the butterfly only as a logo decoration.

The butterfly must have a NARRATIVE ROLE.

---

## What remains creatively open

Impeccable has full creative freedom over HOW the butterfly journey is expressed.

Possible dimensions include:

- chrysalis;
- trace;
- silhouette;
- fragmented form;
- partial wing geometry;
- evolving shape;
- changing scale;
- changing material;
- path;
- trail;
- background interaction;
- light;
- depth;
- layers;
- openings;
- progressive reveal;
- section transitions;
- movement;
- emergence;
- flight.

These are possibilities, not implementation requirements.

The strongest execution may be abstract at some stages.

However, the relationship to the butterfly journey must remain recognizable.

---

## Background as part of the journey

This is especially important.

The butterfly should not simply move ON TOP OF an otherwise unrelated website.

The background / visual environment itself should be allowed to evolve with the transformation.

The page may progress visually from:

contained

→ compressed

→ opening

→ movement

→ air

→ space

→ emergence

The changing environment can be expressed through:

- color;
- light;
- depth;
- scale;
- shape;
- texture;
- negative space;
- transitions;
- composition.

The exact solution is open.

The goal is:

> the journey happens to the whole page, not only to an icon.

---

# Invalid governing directions

Impeccable may NOT replace the butterfly journey with another primary metaphor.

Invalid substitutions include:

- correspondence / letters;
- architecture;
- doors alone;
- generic opening;
- maps;
- topography;
- generic abstract geometry;
- light alone;
- waves;
- nature metaphors unrelated to the butterfly;
- unrelated symbolic systems.

Secondary supporting ideas MAY exist if they strengthen the butterfly journey.

They may never replace it.

---

# Concept-roll override

This direction is explicitly pinned by the user.

It overrides concept-roll alternatives.

Concept exploration should therefore answer:

> "How does the NeoCrisálida butterfly transform and travel through the page?"

It should NOT answer:

> "What completely different metaphor could replace the butterfly?"

Different proposed directions should represent different EXECUTIONS of the butterfly journey.

# Mobile priority

Mobile is a first-class design target.

# Reduced motion

The experience must respect:

`prefers-reduced-motion`

The visual narrative must remain understandable without animation.

Motion can enhance the journey.

Motion cannot be required to understand the content.
