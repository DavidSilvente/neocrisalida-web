## Dawn

URL:

https://www.lapa.ninja/post/joindawn/

Local reference:

`global/dawn-structure.png`

Primary reason:

- whole-site storytelling;
- structural coherence;
- recurring symbolic object;
- making sections feel part of one visual world.

Dawn gives strong importance to the sun.

NeoCrisálida must NOT copy the sun.

The important principle is:

A recurring visual concept can make the entire page feel like a journey.

For NeoCrisálida, explore whether transformation / chrysalis / emergence / butterfly can perform that narrative role.

The BACKGROUND and overall experience may progressively evolve through the page.

Do NOT copy:

- exact layout;
- palette;
- typography;
- sun;
- animations.
